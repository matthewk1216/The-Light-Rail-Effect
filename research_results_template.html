Methods and Validity Checklist

  • [ ] Treatment and control assignment rule written in one paragraph.
  • [ ] Pre-trend check reported (graph + table).
  • [ ] Standard errors and clustering level justified.
  • [ ] At least three robustness checks completed.
  • [ ] Missing data handling documented.
  • [ ] Limitations and likely confounders listed explicitly.
  • [ ] Reproducibility note included (how someone reruns analysis).
Do not overclaim causality when pre-trends fail or robustness checks are unstable. Mark confidence level honestly.

One-Paragraph Abstract Template

[Question] This study estimates whether [treatment] changed [outcome] in [setting] from [start] to [end].
[Design] I use [method] comparing [treated group] to [control group], with [fixed effects/controls].
[Main result] The baseline estimate is [beta] ([SE], 95% CI [lb, ub]), implying [plain-English effect size].
[Credibility] Pre-trends are [supported/not supported], and robustness checks [support/partially support/weaken] the baseline result.
[Takeaway] Findings suggest [policy implication], with key limitations including [top limitations].
